I study algorithms for finding useful patterns in data, building efficient indexes, and protecting privacy—with applications in biomedical informatics and beyond.

Research

From algorithmic foundations
to useful data analysis.

01 / ALGORITHMS & INDEXING

Making large data searchable

String algorithms, compact indexes, pattern matching, and graph problems. Recent work includes uncertain strings, string anchors, and subtree mode queries.

02 / DATA MINING

Discovering meaningful structure

Pattern discovery in strings and time series, dense subgraphs, and network analysis. Connecting efficient methods with applications in scientific and real-world data.

03 / PRIVACY & HEALTH

Learning while protecting people

Privacy-preserving data publishing, anonymization, and differential privacy, including methods for electronic health records and biomedical analysis.

About

Background

I am an Associate Professor (Senior Lecturer) in the Department of Informatics at King's College London. My interests span data mining, data privacy, biomedical informatics, and data science.

Before joining King's, I held an Assistant Professor position at Cardiff University and a Royal Academy of Engineering Research Fellowship (2011–2016). Earlier, I was a postdoctoral researcher in Biomedical Informatics at Vanderbilt University.

I earned my PhD in Computer Science at Cardiff University and my Diploma in Computer Science at the University of Crete. I am a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy and a member of ACM.
